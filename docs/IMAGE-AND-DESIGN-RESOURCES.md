# Image and design resources

Research checked against official pages on 2026-11-17. This is a shortlist, not an assertion that every asset on a platform is cleared. Record the asset's creator, exact page, download date, license copy, releases and intended placement before adding it. No new drama/Netflix stills or actor photographs were downloaded for this expansion.

## Fonts

[Google Fonts licensing FAQ](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq) is the discovery reference; the individual family license is authoritative. Inter, Noto Sans KR, Gowun Batang, Playfair Display and DM Sans are candidates; confirm the family OFL file before redistribution. Existing DM Sans, Gowun Batang and Playfair Display license files are retained locally. Keep their names and notices, do not sell font files alone, and respect any reserved font names for modifications. Use local WOFF2 subsets and complete licensed fallbacks. Inter/Noto Sans KR are researched alternatives, not additional downloads or new network requests.

## Asset policy implemented

Existing Photo/Next Image fallbacks and aspect ratios are reused. New editorial artwork uses original project art; supplied catalog art keeps its existing attribution. A registry identifies editorial artwork and its provenance. Article-specific images and captions can be added only after admission review. Do not label existing TMDB photos commercially licensed until documentation is supplied.
